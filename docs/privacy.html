Ravenarr Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Ravenarr is a client app for your own self-hosted Overseerr or Jellyseerr server. It has no backend of its own, collects no analytics, and no data passes through any server operated by the app's developers.

What the app stores, and where

  • Server address you enter — stored locally on your device (UserDefaults).
  • Session credentials (the cookie returned after you sign in with Plex, Jellyfin/Emby, or a local account) — stored in your device's Keychain, never transmitted anywhere except the server you configured.
  • Plex sign-in happens entirely between your device and Plex's own servers (plex.tv) using Plex's standard PIN login flow. The resulting token is then sent only to the Overseerr/Jellyseerr server you specified, to establish your session there.

Push notifications (optional)

If you enable push notifications and configure a relay URL, your device's APNs push token and your Seerr user ID are sent to that relay — a service you choose to run yourself, not one operated by this app's developers. See the project README for what that relay does with it.

What we don't do

No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. No data is sold, shared, or sent to any server other than the one you explicitly configured.
